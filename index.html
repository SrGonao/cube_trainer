Your drills

How to use it: do the trigger the number of times shown, back to back, with the grip you use in solves. Go for clean and even first; speed comes later. The rhythm spread is how much your turn-to-turn gaps vary: under 25% is even. A turn that is slow in every repetition is usually a regrip.

Targets to aim for over a few weeks: about 5–6 turns per second on the sexy move and the inserts, 4 or more on full algs.

Use your own algs

Paste algs, e.g. copied from J Perm's OLL or PLL page. Put one per line; names and other text on the line are ignored. Slotwise works out which case each alg solves, so the order doesn't matter. The first alg for a case wins. You can also change a single card's alg after solving it.

Tick the cases you know. Next card picks from those, showing the ones you are slowest at and make the most slips on (wrong turns, extra turns, giving up) most often. No grading needed. Click any card to practise it right away.

Your data

Your solves, cards and settings are saved on this device, in this browser. Sign in below to keep your phone and laptop in step. Export a backup now and then; Import brings it back.

Cube MAC address needed

GAN cubes encrypt their data with a key based on the MAC address. You can find it in csTimer's Bluetooth dialog or in the GAN app. It looks like AB:12:CD:34:EF:56.

To run from localhost: open a terminal in the folder with slotwise.html, run python3 -m http.server 8000 (on Windows: py -m http.server 8000), and open http://localhost:8000/slotwise.html in Chrome.
